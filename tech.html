01 / AERO

Aerodynamics

Wings create downforce. The floor and diffuser guide air under the car. DRS opens a rear-wing flap to reduce drag in permitted zones.

02 / POWER

Power unit

The hybrid power unit combines an internal-combustion engine, MGU-K, MGU-H and a battery to deliver and recover energy.

03 / TYRES

Tyres & degradation

Soft, medium and hard compounds trade speed for durability. Heat and wear reduce grip, so tyre management shapes pit strategy.
