01 THE SPORT

One championship.
Twenty drivers.

Formula 1 is the pinnacle of international single-seater motorsport. Across a season of Grand Prix weekends, ten teams design and race extraordinary cars, with two drivers representing each team.

Every result is a mix of raw pace, smart engineering and split-second decisions. Follow the grid, explore the teams, and discover what makes these machines so remarkable.
